{% comment %} A candy's add-to-cart control: one button-shaped bar holding minus, the quantity in a field of its own, and plus. The quantity can be typed over; once it differs from what the cart holds, Ok appears, and Ok or Enter sets it. Included on the catalog and the detail page (place "page") and in the detail popup (place "dialog"), and rendered again for both places by cart_stepper_response.html after every change. Every change is swapped in out of band, by id -- so the ids carry the place, a candy's stepper can be on the page and in the popup at the same time, and a place that is not on the page is skipped silently. hx-swap="none" is inherited by the buttons; there is no in-band content to swap. htmx gives focus back to the element with the same id after the swap. Ok has none, because once the number is set it hides again: before its request, focus moves to the quantity field, which keeps it. The bar itself is what a customer sees, everywhere and from the start: the number says how many are in the cart, so nothing is laid over it saying the same. "Add to cart" lives on as the plus button's accessible name. The field is short and the label around it is tap-high, so a finger landing anywhere beside the box still reaches the field (site.css). Minus with nothing in the cart, and plus with every one in stock already in it, are greyed out with aria-disabled rather than disabled: a disabled button cannot keep focus, so a keyboard user who empties the cart from the stepper would be dropped to the top of the page. Pressing one anyway changes nothing -- and plus still answers with the refusal UC-04 ext. 2a asks for, rather than leaving a press unexplained. A refusal appears as a bubble above the bar rather than as a line in the card, so being told something never moves the bars around it (site.css). No min/max or pattern on the field: htmx runs the browser's validation before posting, which would stop a bad number with a native tooltip and keep the server's message (and its stock cap) from ever showing. {% endcomment %} {% firstof place "page" as where %}{% firstof candy.pk pk as id %}
